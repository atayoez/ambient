The controller is the natural or legal person who alone or jointly with others determines the purposes and means of processing of personal data.

What data is processed

This site is intentionally minimal. Two sources of data:

1. Server logs (Cloudflare)

When you visit this site, our hosting provider Cloudflare automatically records: IP address, requested URL, HTTP method, response status, user-agent string, referrer, timestamp. This is kept for at most 30 days for the purpose of detecting and mitigating attacks, abuse, and operational issues.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the secure operation of this website.

2. Purchase data (when you buy)

The "Buy" button takes you to a checkout hosted by Polar Software, Inc. ("Polar"), our Merchant of Record. Polar collects your payment information, billing country, and email address directly. We receive only: your email address, an order ID, the product purchased, and confirmation of payment. Polar's own privacy policy applies during checkout.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of the contract you enter into when you buy.

Cookies

This site sets no first-party cookies. We do not run analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Posthog, no anything).

Cloudflare may set a strictly-necessary technical cookie (__cf_bm, "bot management") for ~30 minutes for security purposes. Under §25 (2) TDDDG this falls under the consent exception for technically necessary cookies; no banner is required.

Recipients / sub-processors

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — site hosting and CDN. Server-log data may transit and be cached in jurisdictions outside the EEA, including the United States, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework + Cloudflare's GDPR DPA.
  • Polar Software, Inc. — payment processing. Acts as Merchant of Record for purchases. Processes your payment info under their own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your data with any other party.

International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the EEA (chiefly via Cloudflare and Polar), we rely on either an adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR or Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR for the relevant processors.

Your rights under GDPR

  • Access (Art. 15) — confirmation of whether we process data about you and, if so, a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — deletion of your data where the legal grounds for processing no longer exist.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) — limit processing to storage during a dispute.
  • Portability (Art. 20) — export of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent), without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these rights, email atay@oezcan.me. We will respond within one month per Art. 12(3) GDPR.

Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority — typically the one in the federal state where you reside or work. In Germany, see the Bundesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz for the list of state authorities.

Changes

We may update this policy when our practices change. The effective date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
