What's in it

Five pattern generators

Mesh gradients, geometric shapes, dot grids, stripes, and iOS-style emoji wallpapers using Microsoft Fluent 3D. Re-rolls in milliseconds at your screen's exact resolution.

Curated photo sources

One-click pulls from NASA APOD, Unsplash, and Pexels. Free, BYO key for the search-driven providers. Cached locally so the preview is instant.

AI generation

Write a prompt, hit ⌘↩, get a wallpaper applied. Free via Pollinations (no key needed) or bring your OpenAI key for DALL-E 3.

Your own library

Every wallpaper you apply is saved locally. Re-apply with a click, delete with a second click. Lives in ~/.local/share; never leaves your machine.

Local. Private. Yours.

Built on Tauri + Rust + React. No telemetry, no accounts, no servers. Every wallpaper you make stays on your machine — nothing leaves the device without your say-so.

Designed like an Apple inspector

Liquid-glass surfaces, intrinsic-width sliders, sticky preview column. Native Wayland on Linux, vibrancy on macOS, Mica on Windows.
