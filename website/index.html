What's in it

Ten pattern generators

Mesh gradients, dots, stripes, geometric shapes, flowing lines, waves, aurora, bokeh, petals, and emoji walls. Ten of them. Tweak the knobs or hit shuffle until one clicks.

Curated photo sources

A fresh photo daily from NASA and Bing, plus random shots from Lorem Picsum, all free with no key. Add a free Unsplash, Pexels, or Wallhaven key if you want to search. Everything you apply gets saved locally.

AI generation

Type a prompt, hit ⌘↩. Pollinations works for free with no setup. Add a token to lift the rate limit, or use your own OpenAI key for DALL·E 3.

Your own library

Everything you've used hangs around in the library. One click puts it back on the desktop, two clicks delete it. The files just sit on your disk.

Stays on your machine

Tauri + Rust + React under the hood. No account, no telemetry, no backend at all. The only thing the app calls out to is whichever photo source you've turned on.

Designed like an Apple inspector

Inspector on the right, preview on the left that stays put while you tweak. Glass surfaces, minimal motion. Native Wayland on Linux.
